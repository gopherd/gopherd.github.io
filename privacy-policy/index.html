1. About this policy

Gopherd is the name we use as independent developers of utility apps. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the independent developers behind Gopherd.

This policy describes information handling for gopherd.com, communications with us, and apps that link to this policy. An app may provide an additional privacy notice describing its specific features, permissions, and service providers. Please read that notice alongside this policy; the more specific notice applies to the practices it describes.

2. Information we handle

When you visit this website. This site provides information and links to our contact email. It does not offer accounts or collect information through forms. We do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies on this website. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, and access times, to deliver and secure the site.

When you contact us. We receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or details you choose to send. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

When you use an app. The information involved depends on the features you use. An app’s privacy notice and permission prompts explain any access to device features or personal information. Granting a device permission does not, by itself, mean that information is sent to us. Refer to the app’s notice for whether information stays on your device or is transmitted to a service provider.

When you make a purchase. If an app offers purchases or subscriptions, the app store or payment provider handles payment information under its own privacy policy. We may receive transaction or entitlement information needed to provide the purchased features, but do not receive your full payment card details from app store purchases.

3. How information is used

We use information we receive to respond to questions, provide support, deliver purchased features where applicable, troubleshoot problems, protect our services from misuse, and meet legal obligations. Any additional app-specific processing is described in the applicable app notice.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide a service you request, support our legitimate interests in maintaining secure and reliable services, comply with a legal obligation, or act on your consent. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

4. Sharing and service providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes information associated with hosting and delivering the website as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We also use an email provider to receive and respond to support messages. These providers may process information needed to provide their services. App stores process purchases under their own terms and policies. Additional providers used by a particular app are described in that app’s notice.

We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate misuse, or defend legal rights. We do not sell information received through this website or support correspondence.

5. Retention and security

We keep support correspondence and related information for as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request, maintain relevant records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and type of information. App-specific retention practices are described in the relevant app notice.

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please keep your devices and accounts secure as well.

6. Your choices and rights

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy of information, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. We may need to verify your request before acting on it, and legal exceptions may apply.

You can manage app permissions in your device settings. Deleting an app may remove locally stored information, but does not necessarily delete information held by an online service or cancel an app store subscription. Contact us if you need help with information we hold.

7. Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For apps, follow the age requirements and any specific privacy notice provided with the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without the required permission, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. International processing

Hosting, email, and other service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Applicable legal requirements govern any international transfers of personal information we arrange, including safeguards where required.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or information practices change. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice or request consent when required by applicable law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email i@mkideal.com. Please include the name of the app, if relevant, and enough information for us to understand your request.
